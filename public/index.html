      What is an AI rap video generator?+
      Turn a topic or your own lyrics and one or two photos into a short lip-sync rap video with an original beat.
      Can I use my own lyrics?+
      Yes. Paste your lines, then review six clean lines before paying. Unsafe content is refused and ordinary profanity is rewritten clean. Wording can change to fit the beat.
      Can my AI rapper be a pet or character?+
      Yes. Use your pet, an original character, yourself or an adult who agreed. No children or celebrities.
      What do I get?+
      A 15-second vertical HD MP4 with sound. An AI rap song with six clean lines, lip-sync and a choice of three original beats.
      How much does it cost?+
      1 video for $9.99, 3 for $19.99, or 5 for $29.99. No subscription. Confirmed failures return the video credit.
      How long do you keep my photos?+
      Photos are auto-deleted after 7 days, or you can delete them right away. Finished videos stay available for 30 days.
