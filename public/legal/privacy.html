4. Third-party processors

To deliver a video, your uploads are transmitted to the following categories of third party:

  • AI text generation provider — receives topic and style text to write lyrics.
  • AI music generation provider — receives lyrics to produce vocals and instrumental.
  • AI video generation provider — receives the photo and audio to produce the lip-synced video.
  • Hosting and storage provider — stores files transiently and serves downloads.

Each processor is contractually bound to process data only on our instructions, not to use it for their own purposes, and to delete it. We will maintain a current named list on request.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data; to restrict or object to processing; and to withdraw consent. Regardless of location, you may ask us to delete any photo you uploaded at any time and we will act immediately.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@rapvideo.xyz. We respond within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied, you may complain to your national data protection authority; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Security

We use transport encryption in transit and at rest, restrict internal access to uploaded media on a need-to-know basis, and log administrative access. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify you and the relevant authority of a breach where legally required.

10. Children

RapVideo is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Uploading a photo of a minor is prohibited under our Terms of Service. If we learn that we hold data relating to a minor we will delete it immediately and terminate the associated account.

11. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to process checkout, and optional analytics cookies to understand aggregate usage. Analytics cookies are set only with your consent where consent is required. You can block cookies in your browser; doing so may break login and checkout.

12. No automated decision-making about you

We use AI to generate creative content at your request. We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not profile you for marketing.
