Refund Policy

How we handle refunds for credits and generated videos at RapVideo.

The short version. Unused credits: full refund within 14 days. A credit already spent on a finished video: not refundable, because the cost has been incurred and cannot be recovered. Failed or non-delivered generation: always refunded, no questions asked.

1. What you are buying

You buy the right to have us generate AI music videos on your behalf. Each completed video consumes one credit. Because each generation incurs real, non-recoverable computing cost at the moment it runs, our refund terms differ depending on whether that cost has been incurred.

2. Refund eligibility

SituationOutcome
Unused credits, within 14 days of purchaseFull refund
Generation failed, errored, or was never deliveredCredit returned automatically, or full refund if you prefer
Output quality below your expectations, video deliveredNot refunded as cash; we will re-run the generation for you
Credits used successfullyNot refunded
Used credits after the 14-day windowNot refunded
Account terminated for breaching our Terms of ServiceNot refunded
Service discontinued by usProportional refund of unused credits

3. Our quality commitment

We accept that AI output is variable and sometimes poor. If a delivered video is clearly defective — unintelligible audio, wrong number of people, visible corruption, or failure to follow your topic — contact us within 7 days and we will re-generate it at no charge. If we cannot produce an acceptable result after two attempts, we will refund that credit in full.

4. How to request a refund

Email support@rapvideo.xyz with the subject "Refund request" and include:

  • the email address used for the purchase,
  • the date of purchase,
  • the order or receipt reference, and
  • a short description of the issue.

We acknowledge every request within 2 business days and resolve it within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, the money may take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

5. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. We resolve legitimate requests faster and more reliably than the chargeback process, and an unnecessary chargeback may result in suspension of your account. This does not affect your statutory rights.

6. Statutory rights

Consumers in the European Union and the United Kingdom have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance purchase within 14 days. Where the service has already been performed at your explicit request, that right does not extend to the portion already delivered. Nothing in this policy reduces rights you have under mandatory consumer law in your country of residence.

Billing and refund questions: support@rapvideo.xyz
